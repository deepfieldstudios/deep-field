Website redesign

Rebuilt from the ground up

No themes, no page-builders, no platform to rent. Native code you own, fast because of how it is built rather than what was bolted on afterwards.

01Why redesign

Most sites are slow because of what they are built on, not what is on them.

Stuck on a platform

Your site lives inside someone else's system. You cannot edit it, move it, or take it with you — and the search history you have built up sits in addresses you do not control.

Slow on a phone

A page-builder ships a database, a theme, a dozen plugins and megabytes of JavaScript before a visitor sees anything. Most people leave before it finishes.

Paying to stand still

Licences, subscriptions and plugin renewals, every year, for a site that is read far more often than it is edited.

02How it works
