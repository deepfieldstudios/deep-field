Case study 01 · Hospitality

Jungle Bay Dominica

An award-winning eco-resort whose website took 41 seconds to show anything on a phone, and which it did not own.

Problem

The site was built and hosted by a hospitality marketing vendor. Jungle Bay could not edit it, move it, or take it with them — and fifteen years of accumulated search history sat inside addresses only that vendor controlled. On mobile it was effectively unusable: an 8 MB hero video loading before anything appeared.

Approach

Rather than copy the old site, we extracted only what belonged to Jungle Bay — their words and their photographs — and discarded every trace of the vendor's markup. Boilerplate was detected by how often it repeated, so nothing about the old platform's structure was carried across. A verification step fails the build outright if a vendor string survives.

Result

A site eighteen times faster on mobile, weighing 270 KB with 1.9 KB of JavaScript where there had been three separate frameworks. Every one of the 77 canonical addresses still resolves, so no ranking was lost in the move. The resort owns the code, the hosting and the domain outright.

Case study 02 · Coaching

Freeflow Dynamics

A seventeen-time US national record holder, coaching without a home of her own on the web.

Live at
freeflowdynamics.com
Scope
Design & build, five pages, booking integration
Stack
Static HTML, GitHub Pages
Launched
June 2026

Problem

Natalie Bruce's record and her coaching practice existed only across social profiles she did not control. Prospective clients had nowhere to understand the programs, or to book.

Approach

Photography-led and deliberately quiet — her images do the persuading. Programs laid out so the decision is obvious, with booking handled inline rather than pushed to email.

Result

The studio's first client site, and the pattern every build since has followed: custom design, static output, hosting that costs nothing, and full ownership handed over at launch.

Case study 03 · Sport

Blue Element

An international freediving competition and training centre in Dominica, moving off WordPress.

Live at
blueelementstore.com
Scope
Design & build, store, WordPress replacement
Stack
Static HTML, GitHub Pages
Launched
August 2026

Problem

A WordPress install carrying plugin licences, update risk and hosting cost, for a site that is read far more often than it is edited.

Approach

Rebuilt as plain static pages — no database, no admin panel, no plugins to keep patched. Course and competition information structured so a season's changes are a small edit rather than a rebuild.

Result

A faster site with no recurring platform cost and no security surface to maintain.
